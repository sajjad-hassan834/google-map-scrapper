Help & guide

🚀 Getting started

  1. Search format: Use targeted queries like [niche] in [city, state] (e.g., painters in Tampa FL or roofers in Dallas TX). Broad nationwide queries return only corporate headquarters.
  2. Lead filters: Keep "No website only" enabled to automatically identify businesses operating without an active web presence.
  3. Opportunity tiers: Businesses with 15+ Google reviews and no website are marked as HOT LEADS because they already have established customers but are leaking search traffic.
  4. Save to CRM: Click "Save to CRM" to add qualified prospects to your personal lead tracker.
  5. Pipeline stages: Move saved leads through New Lead → Contacted → Audit Sent → In Discussion → Closed Won.

⚡ How credits work

  • Fast search: Dispatches requests to the Google Places API (New). Each page of up to 20 businesses uses 1 credit. A 3-page search uses 3 credits.
  • Monthly free tier: Google provides 1,000 free requests per calendar month on the Text Search Enterprise SKU.
  • Timezone reset: Usage counters automatically reset to zero on the 1st of each month according to Pacific Time (PT), matching Google's billing cycle.
  • 30-day cache: Repeating an identical search within 30 days costs 0 credits and is served instantly from local storage.
  • Deep search: Runs the local Docker browser scraper and uses 0 Google credits (completely free and unlimited).
💡 Tip: You can select 1 page (quick) before searching to quickly inspect a city niche for just 1 credit.

🤝 Contacting leads responsibly

  • Call first: A polite phone call to the owner or office manager converts significantly better than cold emails for local service contractors.
  • Be personalized: Mention specific details from their Google listing (e.g. "I noticed you have 48 five-star reviews on Google, but your listing doesn't link to a mobile website").
  • Respect anti-spam laws: Follow CAN-SPAM and applicable local laws. Always provide your legal business name, physical contact address, and a clear opt-out.
  • Immediate opt-out: If an owner indicates they are not interested, mark the lead as Lost or delete it immediately and never reach out again.
